Who runs the game

minds.games, powered by Animoca Brands; Minds is by Animoca Brands. To contact us about your data, use the 🐞 Report button in the game's Settings.

What we collect

DataWhy

Display name and profile pictureTo show you to other players
Game data: island, items, Gems, matches, raids, ratings, Town positionTo run the game

Cheat flags and admin actionsTo keep the game fair

We do not ask for your name, email address or wallet secret phrase. Never share your secret phrase with anyone.

Public information

Blockchain activity is public and permanent. Your display name, profile picture, ratings, match history and a shortened wallet address can be seen by other players.

Stored in your browser

The game keeps your session, settings and recent Mind conversations in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising cookies.

Services we use

  • Vercel: hosting
  • Supabase: database and live connections
  • Robinhood Chain public network: reading wallets and payments
  • Google Fonts: delivers the game's fonts, and receives your IP address when loading them
  • GitHub: runs our scheduled jobs and keeps encrypted database backups for 30 days
  • Your wallet provider, under its own terms
  • If you connect an AI, that AI's provider receives game data for your account
  • Hello Minds (Animoca Brands): if you connect your Hello Minds account, your AI Minds live there. The game sends them questions with your game state (island, plan, duel situation) and reads their answers, using your Hello Minds login and your own cognition. Your browser's Hello Minds login is used for each request only and not kept. If you turn on Away play, the game keeps a separate encrypted Hello Minds sign-in until Away play ends or you turn it off.

How long we keep data

Friend, duel and private chat, finished duels and mail: 30 days. Mind Town chat: you only see it while you are in town; the server keeps it 1 day for reports. Reports and anti-cheat notes: 60 days. Raid logs: the newest 20. Purchase history (what you bought with Dust or Gems, and Gem packs with their transaction): 30 days. A daily copy of your island, so support can restore it: 7 days. Trial accounts not used for 30 days are deleted. Gem payment records: 7 years, for tax and accounting. Encrypted backups: 30 days. Other account data: while your account exists.

Pot duels and trades: what you put in and what you got are kept in your island history (the newest 30) and in our records of the trade.

Why we may use your data (legal basis)

To run the game you signed up for (contract); to keep the game fair and secure, fix bugs and improve it (legitimate interests); to keep payment records (legal obligation). Optional things like Away play happen only when you turn them on.

Where data is kept

Our database is in London (UK). Our hosting and other services may handle data in other countries, including the United States, under those providers' standard data protection terms.

Your choices and rights

You can change your display name, turn off AI keys and Away play, and log out of all devices in Settings. You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how we use it: send us a 🐞 Report and we will answer within 30 days. If you are in the UK or EU you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO). Records on the blockchain cannot be deleted.

Children

The game is not for anyone under 18 (you confirm this when you sign up and before your first purchase).

Security

We protect data with access rules, signed sessions and rate limits. No system is perfectly secure.

Changes

We may update this policy and will show the new date above.
