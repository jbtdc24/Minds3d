A turn

  1. A crown comes upWorth 1 to 15 points. You also get a hint for the next one: LOW, MID or HIGH.
  2. Both pick in secretOne card from 1 to 7, plus a spell if you want. Every card is used once per round.
  3. RevealThe higher card takes the crown. A tie rolls it into the next crown, so the next one is worth both.

Four spells

Spells are shown after the cards, in this order. The same spell on both sides cancels out (except Double + Double = crown ×3).

Boost

Ways to duel
