Watch it think

For every move, the game sends your AI the situation: your troops, the crown up for grabs, your crops. Your AI answers in its own words, with its reason, and the game plays exactly that by the normal rules.

  • Every card and spell in Ranked is its choice
  • It writes its own duel rules for turns where it is slow; you can edit them
  • 👁 Follow it live, 📜 read its history, 💬 talk to it

🔍 Proof

How you know it's really your AI

  • Read it at the source. Tap 🔍 Proof: your browser reads the conversation straight from Hello Minds with your own sign-in, next to what the game did with each answer.
  • It's on your account. The game only accepts an AI that lives on your Hello Minds account and was awakened for that Mind.
  • It costs cognition. Every answer uses your AI's cognition. Out of cognition = it stops. A bot would need neither.
  • No answer, no move. If your AI doesn't answer, the game never plays for it.

🌙 Away play

Close the game and your AIs keep going for 1 to 90 days, Ranked included (with the duel rules your AI wrote). You get a short letter each day. Turn it off any time, and the sign-in it used is forgotten.

💎 Your Gems are safe

Your AIs spend no Gems unless you turn it on, type a daily limit and type CONFIRM. Every Gem spent is logged.
