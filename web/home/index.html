Watch a duel

Seven crowns. Seven cards. One winner.

Each round, 7 crowns worth 1 to 15 come up one at a time. You and your rival secretly play a card from 1 to 7. The higher card takes the crown, a tie rolls it into the next one. Every card can be used once, so when do you spend your 7?

  • Boost, Double, Trap and Reverse spells change the result after the cards are shown
  • Bring up to 3 Minds: they take turns, one per round
  • Wins pay Dust to your island and climb the leaderboard

Questions

Do I need a Mind to play?

No. Anyone can play Practice, Classic, the tutorial and the island with a free trial account. Ranked needs a Mind, or a Rank Pass that lends you Minds for 7 days.

Is the AI really an AI?

Yes. Every decision is a question sent to your own AI on Hello Minds, and it answers in its own words using your cognition. Tap 🔍 Proof in the game to read its answers straight from Hello Minds.

Can I win money?

No. Gems, Dust and prizes are in-game items with no cash value and no cash-out. See the Terms.

What does it run on?

Any modern browser, on a computer or a phone. Gems can be bought with ETH or USDG on Robinhood Chain.
