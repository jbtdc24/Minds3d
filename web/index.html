HOW TO PLAY

3 vs 3. Pick 3 MDGs. The first slot stands in front and takes most hits.

Energy. You start with 3 and get +2 every round (max 10). Unused energy carries over.

Cards. Every round each fighter draws 2 cards from its deck. Every trait gives a card: face, hand item, headwear and emote. Everyone also has Bonk. Legendaries get 2 signature cards.

Combo. Play 2 cards on the same fighter and the second hits 15% harder.

Speed. When you press END TURN, everyone acts in speed order. Rarity sets HP, ATK, speed and crit.

🔥 Blaze beats 🌿 Bloom🌿 Bloom beats 💧 Tide💧 Tide beats 🔥 Blaze

Skin colour sets the class: warm = Blaze, green = Bloom, cool = Tide. Legendaries are ✦ Mythic.

Status. 💫 stun skips a turn · 🔥 burn hurts each round · 🛡 shield absorbs damage · 👻 dodge avoids hits · 🎯 taunt pulls attacks.

Win by knocking out all 3 rivals. From round 10, sudden death hurts everyone. Wins give XP; fighters level up to 10.
